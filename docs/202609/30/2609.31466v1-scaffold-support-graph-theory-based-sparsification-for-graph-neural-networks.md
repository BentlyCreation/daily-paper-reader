## 摘要
图神经网络（GNN）依赖沿图边进行消息传递，因此其计算和内存开销与图的密度密切相关。图稀疏化提供了一种降低这些开销的自然途径，但无差别地删除边会扭曲重要的通信结构并降低预测性能。我们提出 Scaffold，一种基于拓扑的无监督图稀疏化框架，源自支撑图理论预条件子。Scaffold 显式控制两个互补的结构量：膨胀率（dilation），衡量由删除边引起的重路由路径长度；拥塞度（congestion），衡量这些重路由路径在保留支撑上的集中程度。通过联合控制膨胀率和拥塞度，Scaffold 在保留短通信路径的同时避免结构瓶颈。据我们所知，Scaffold 是首个使用支撑路径膨胀-拥塞联合准则的可扩展 GNN 稀疏化框架。在涵盖从小图到大图的 19 个同配和异配基准上，Scaffold 在评估的稀疏化及相关方法中取得了最佳综合排名。每个稀疏支撑仅使用原始边的 10%-50%，Scaffold 就能恢复或接近全图 GNN 的性能，同时使用的内存不到全图训练的一半，并缩短了端到端训练时间（包括稀疏化开销）。我们提供开源软件包：https://github.com/siddhartha047/Scaffold。

## Abstract
Graph neural networks (GNNs) rely on message passing over graph edges, making their computational and memory costs strongly dependent on graph density. Graph sparsification offers a natural way to reduce these costs, but removing edges indiscriminately can distort important communication structure and degrade predictive performance. We introduce Scaffold, a topology-based, unsupervised graph sparsification framework derived from support graph theory preconditioners. Scaffold explicitly controls two complementary structural quantities: dilation, which measures the length of rerouting paths induced by removed edges, and congestion, which measures how strongly these rerouted paths concentrate on the retained support. By jointly controlling dilation and congestion, Scaffold preserves short communication paths while avoiding structural bottlenecks. To our knowledge, Scaffold is the first scalable GNN sparsification framework to use a joint supporting-path dilation-congestion criterion. Across 19 homophilic and heterophilic benchmarks spanning small to large graphs, Scaffold achieves the best aggregate rank among the evaluated sparsification and related methods. Using only 10%-50% of the original edges per sparse support, Scaffold recovers or closely approaches full-graph GNN performance while using less than half the memory of full-graph training and reducing end-to-end training time, including sparsification overhead. We provide an open-source software package at https://github.com/siddhartha047/Scaffold.
